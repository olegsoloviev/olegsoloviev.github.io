Chess Widget

Chess Puzzles on your Home Screen, for iPhone and iPad.

Chess Widget is the first widget in the world that shows chess puzzles, right to your Home Screen.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Add the Chess Widget to your Home Screen
  • Every day a new puzzle will appear right on your Chess Widget
  • To solve a chess puzzle, tap into the widget, make the right moves, and then a new puzzle appears on your Home Screen again

    • No internet needed, solve thousands of puzzles anywhere!

    Supported languages: English, 中文, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, 日本語, Português, Русский, Española, Türkçe, Український, Pilipinas
